    Model notes & simplifications Real incubations last minutes; here they are compressed (~2 min total). Molecule sizes, counts and distances are exaggerated. The cabinet is a generic floor-standing immunoassay analyzer inspired by mid-size platforms; it is not a replica of any manufacturer’s product. HRP + luminol/H₂O₂ is shown; other CLIA formats use acridinium esters (alkaline H₂O₂ trigger, flash kinetics) or ALP + AMPPD (glow kinetics). Photometric absorbance is illustrative.
